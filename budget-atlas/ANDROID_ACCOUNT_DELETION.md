# Account and data deletion — Budget Atlas

**App:** Budget Atlas  
**Developer:** Pradap Pandiyan  
**Support:** support@pradappandiyan.com  

This page is for Google Play and App Store account-deletion requirements.

---

## How to request that your account is deleted

### Option 1 — Email (always available)

1. Send an email to **support@pradappandiyan.com**
2. Use the **same email address** as your Budget Atlas account
3. Subject line: `Delete my Budget Atlas account`
4. In the body, write that you want the account and associated data deleted

We will confirm and delete the account. Requests are normally handled within **30 days**.

### Option 2 — In the app

1. Open Budget Atlas and sign in  
2. Go to **Profile**  
3. If you only want some data gone, delete it there (transactions, receipts, wallets, etc.)  
4. To remove the whole account, email support as above (in-app “delete account” may be added later)

---

## What is deleted

When we delete your account we remove or irreversibly anonymize:

- Your login (email, name, password hash, Google/Apple link)  
- Wallets, transactions, budgets, bills, goals, investments, debts  
- Receipt images you uploaded  
- Family / split group memberships you own or belong to (as they relate to you)  
- Screen-usage events tied to your user ID  
- Session tokens  

---

## What may be kept for a limited time

- **Backups** of our database may retain a copy until the next backup cycle (typically days, not more than **90 days**)  
- **Purchase records** Apple or Google keep under their policies (we cannot erase App Store / Play billing history)  
- **Legal / fraud** records if we are required by law to retain them  

We do **not** keep your finance data to sell or to use for ads.

---

## Delete some data without deleting the account

Yes. While signed in you can delete individual items in the app, including:

- Transactions and receipts  
- Wallets, budgets, bills, goals, investments, debts  
- Split groups / family groups you have permission to change  

That does **not** close the account. For a full wipe, use the email request above.

---

## After deletion

You will not be able to sign in. A new account with the same email is a new empty account, not a restore of old data.
