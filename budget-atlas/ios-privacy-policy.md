# Privacy Policy — Budget Atlas

**Last updated:** 13 August 2026  
**Applies to:** Budget Atlas on iOS and Android (`com.pradappandiyan.budgetplanner`)

**Contact:** support@pradappandiyan.com  
**Operator:** Pradap Pandiyan (individual / sole developer)

---

## 1. Who we are

Budget Atlas is a personal finance app for tracking wallets, transactions, budgets, bills, goals, investments, debts, reports, and shared household groups. It is mobile-only.

We do **not** connect to your bank. You enter money data yourself (or from receipts you choose to attach).

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account

- Email address
- Name (if you provide it)
- Password (stored hashed; we do not keep your plain password)
- Sign-in with Google (email and basic profile from the Google token)
- Sign in with Apple on iOS (Apple may hide your email behind a private relay)
- Session / device information needed to keep you logged in

### 2.2 Financial data you enter

- Wallets and balances
- Transactions (amount, date, category, notes, tags)
- Budgets, bills, recurring items
- Goals, investments, debts
- Categories, templates, exchange rates you set
- Split groups and family groups (including invites by email)
- Exports and reports you generate (Premium / Family features)

This data is stored on our servers so it can sync across your devices and, if you choose, with people you invite.

### 2.3 Receipts and photos

If you attach a receipt:

- Camera or photo-library images you choose
- Receipt files uploaded to our servers and linked to a transaction
- On-device text recognition (OCR) to help fill amount/merchant. Processing happens on the device; we store the image if you save or upload it

### 2.4 Voice commands

If you use voice input, the microphone is used while you speak a command. Speech is used on device to create a transaction. We do not build a voice profile.

### 2.5 Security features (mostly on-device)

- App PIN and biometric unlock (Face ID / fingerprint) stay on the device; we do not receive your PIN or biometric templates
- Optional two-factor authentication (TOTP) uses a secret stored for your account

### 2.6 Subscriptions and payments

Purchases go through Apple or Google, not through a card form in our app. We use RevenueCat to know which plan you have.

We may store subscription tier and status, store (iOS / Android), renewal or expiry time, and identifiers Apple, Google, or RevenueCat send in purchase events. We do not store your full card number.

### 2.7 Product analytics

We record screen views (which screens you open) tied to your account so we can understand product usage. This is our own backend telemetry, not a third-party ads SDK.

### 2.8 Crash and diagnostics

If crash reporting is enabled, we may receive crash logs, device type, OS version, and stack traces. We do not sell this data.

### 2.9 Technical data

- App version, device OS
- Approximate time of requests
- IP address as seen by our hosting provider (typical for any HTTPS API)

### 2.10 What we do not collect

- Bank login credentials or automatic bank feeds
- Precise GPS location
- Advertising IDs for ads (Budget Atlas does not show third-party ads)

## 3. How we use information

- Provide the app (sync, login, your data)
- Invite others to family / split groups when you ask
- Send transactional email (welcome, password reset, invites)
- Enforce subscription limits and restore purchases
- Improve the product (screen usage, crash fixes)
- Secure accounts and prevent abuse
- Comply with law and store-review requirements

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your financial entries to train public AI models.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | Scan receipts and barcodes |
| Photo library | Attach an existing receipt image |
| Microphone | Voice commands to add transactions |
| Biometrics / device credentials | Unlock the app with Face ID / fingerprint / PIN |
| Notifications | Local bill / reminder notifications you enable |
| Internet | Sync with our API |

You can deny a permission; related features will not work until you allow it in Settings.

## 5. Sharing

| Party | Why |
|---|---|
| Apple / Google | Sign-in, in-app purchases, store compliance |
| RevenueCat | Subscription status and purchase events |
| Email provider | Password reset, welcome, invites |
| Hosting | Store accounts and your finance data |
| Crash reporting (if enabled) | Crash reports |
| People you invite | Only the group data you share |

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service.

We do **not** sell data to data brokers or use it for third-party advertising.

## 6. International processing

Servers may be in regions other than where you live. By using the app you understand your data may be processed in those locations.

---

## 7. Retention

- Account and finance data: until you delete the account or ask us to delete it
- Receipt images: until you delete them or the account
- Screen analytics: retained for product analysis for a limited period
- Backups: may persist for a short time after deletion as part of normal backup cycles (not more than 90 days)

---

## 8. Your choices

- Access and edit your data in the app
- Export (Premium / Family, where the product allows)
- Delete receipts, transactions, and other records you created
- Sign out; revoke Google / Apple access in those accounts’ settings
- Delete your account in the app: **Profile → Delete account**, or email **support@pradappandiyan.com**. See [Account deletion](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md).

---

## 9. Children

Budget Atlas is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 10. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Protect your device PIN and account password.
