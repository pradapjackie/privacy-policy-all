Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 September 2026 · Platform: iOS

Indru (இன்று — Tamil for “today”), also listed as WhatsToday, is a bilingual on-this-day history app. Favourites, settings, feedback, and the history archive stay on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate a backend that receives your personal data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Indru (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the iOS application published under bundle ID com.pradappandiyan.whatstoday.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store locally:

  • Favourites you save
  • In-app feedback you write (rating, category, and message) — this stays on the device and is not sent to us unless you email us separately
  • App preferences such as content language (English, Tamil, or both), onboarding completion, and whether the daily reminder is enabled
  • History content shipped with the App, plus extra public facts fetched for dates you open

This information is stored in on-device storage (local database / preferences). It is not uploaded to our servers because the App does not provide cloud sync or remote accounts.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No payment card details collected by us (any App Store purchases are handled by Apple)
  • No advertising identifier collection for ad targeting
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that identify you
  • No sale of personal information

4. Network use

The App works offline with its curated archive. The first time you open a calendar date that is not already stored, it may make a best-effort request to Wikipedia’s public “on this day” feed and Wikidata to add extra public facts for that date. Those requests do not include your name, email, or Apple ID. If the request fails, curated content still shows.

Wikipedia and Wikidata content is used under their respective licences (including CC BY-SA 4.0 where applicable). Their processing of network requests is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation’s policies.

5. Optional features that leave the App

  • Contact us: Opens your device’s Mail app with a message to support@pradappandiyan.com. Email you send is processed as ordinary correspondence.
  • Share / export: Uses your device’s share sheet or clipboard when you share an event, image card, PDF, or CSV. Files are created on device at your request.
  • Open source article: May open Wikipedia or another source link in Safari. That visit is governed by the destination site’s policy.

6. Permissions & Apple privacy labels

The App may request notification permission only if you turn on the daily reminder. That reminder is scheduled locally on your device; there is no push-notification server. The App does not require access to Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, or Photos for core features.

Data you save is used only to provide App functionality on your device. App Store privacy nutrition labels should reflect that we do not collect user data for tracking. Optional Wikipedia fetches retrieve public encyclopaedia content, not your personal information.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience history companion and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because data stays on your device, you control retention. Deleting the App removes locally stored App data from that device, subject to any device backups you maintain (for example iCloud device backups you enable separately).

9. Third-party services

Distribution, optional public-history fetches, and optional review flows may involve:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy

10. International users

The App processes data locally on your device. If you contact us by email, that correspondence may be handled in the country where we operate our support inbox.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
