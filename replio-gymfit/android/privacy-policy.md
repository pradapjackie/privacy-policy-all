# Replio GymFit Privacy Policy (Android)

**Last updated:** 18 August 2026  
**Applies to:** Replio GymFit on **Android** (Google Play)  
**Package name:** `com.pradappandiyan.gymworkout`

This Privacy Policy explains what information Replio GymFit (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Android app, you agree to this policy.

> This document describes how the app works today. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Who we are

Replio GymFit is a gym workout tracker operated by Pradap Pandiyan (individual / sole developer).

**Contact / support:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)

## 2. How the app stores data

Replio GymFit is **local-first**. Workout logs, programs, nutrition, measurements, and photos you add are stored **on your device**. We do **not** create an online account, and we do **not** operate a cloud backend that receives your gym data.

If you email support, we will see the information you include in that email.

## 3. Information we handle

Depending on how you use the app, the following may be stored on your device:

### Workout and fitness content you create
- Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest timers, and session history  
- Programs and training plans  
- Nutrition and water logs  
- Body measurements  
- Optional body / equipment profile details you enter (for example height, weight, or available equipment)  
- Progress photos you take or pick from your gallery  

### Settings
- Theme, units, rest defaults, and reminder preferences  

### Permissions you allow
- **Camera** — to take progress photos  
- **Photos / media** — to attach an existing progress photo  
- **Notifications** — optional local workout timer alerts and a daily reminder you enable  
- **Microphone** — declared because the app plays timer sounds through the Android audio system. We do **not** record, store, or upload your voice  
- We do **not** use precise device location  

### Device and store data we do not collect ourselves
Google Play / Android may collect standard diagnostics or crash reports according to your device settings. We do not run third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in Replio GymFit.

## 4. How we use information

On-device data is used only to:

- Show your workouts, history, nutrition, measurements, and photos in the app  
- Play rest-timer sounds and show local reminders you turn on  
- Let you export a backup or CSV files you choose to share  

**We do not sell your personal information.**  
**We do not use your data for third-party advertising.**  
**We do not track you across other apps or websites.**

## 5. Sharing of information

We do not upload your gym data to our servers.

You may choose to share a **local backup file** or CSV export through Android’s share sheet (for example to Files, Drive, or email). That copy is then under the service you pick.

We may see personal data only if you send it to support, or if required by law.

Google Play services may process technical data under Google’s privacy policy.

## 6. Data retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall Replio GymFit.

- Delete individual items in the app where that option exists  
- **Profile → Reset all data** wipes local app data and restores the default exercise library  
- Uninstalling the app removes local app storage on that device (unless you previously exported a backup)

Backup files you saved outside the app remain until you delete them.

## 7. Security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage on the device. Protect your phone with a lock screen. No method of storage is 100% secure. Keep backup files in a place you trust.

## 8. Children’s privacy

Replio GymFit is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, including Google Play child-account rules). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Your choices

- Edit or delete workouts, logs, and photos in the app  
- Export or import a local backup from **Profile**  
- Turn off reminders in **Profile**  
- Control camera, photos, notifications, and microphone in Android **Settings → Apps → Replio GymFit → Permissions**  
- Reset or uninstall to remove local data  
- Email [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com) with privacy questions  

See also: [Data deletion]({{ site.baseurl }}/replio-gymfit/data-deletion/).

## 10. Google Play disclosures

This policy supports Google Play **User Data** / privacy policy requirements. Replio GymFit does not sell personal data and does not use your personal data for third-party advertising. The app does not create a developer-hosted user account.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the updated version at this URL and change the “Last updated” date above.

## 12. Contact

Questions about privacy: [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)
