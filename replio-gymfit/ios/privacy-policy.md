# Replio GymFit Privacy Policy (iOS)

**Last updated:** 18 August 2026  
**Applies to:** Replio GymFit on **iOS** (Apple App Store)  
**Bundle ID:** `com.pradappandiyan.gymworkout`

This Privacy Policy explains what information Replio GymFit (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the iOS app, you agree to this policy.

> This document describes how the app works today. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Who we are

Replio GymFit is a gym workout tracker operated by Pradap Pandiyan (individual / sole developer).

**Contact / support:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)

## 2. How the app stores data

Replio GymFit is **local-first**. Workout logs, programs, nutrition, measurements, and photos you add are stored **on your device**. We do **not** create an online account, and we do **not** operate a cloud backend that receives your gym data.

If you email support, we will see the information you include in that email.

## 3. Information we handle

Depending on how you use the app, the following may be stored on your device:

### Workout and fitness content you create
- Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest timers, and session history  
- Programs and training plans  
- Nutrition and water logs  
- Body measurements  
- Optional body / equipment profile details you enter  
- Progress photos you take or pick from your photo library  

### Settings
- Theme, units, rest defaults, and reminder preferences  

### Permissions you allow
- **Camera** — to take progress photos  
- **Photo Library** — to attach an existing progress photo  
- **Notifications** — optional local workout timer alerts and a daily reminder you enable  
- **Microphone** — may appear because the app plays timer sounds. We do **not** record, store, or upload your voice  
- We do **not** use Location Services  

### Device and store data we do not collect ourselves
Apple may collect standard diagnostics or analytics according to your iOS privacy settings. We do not run third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in Replio GymFit.

## 4. How we use information

On-device data is used only to:

- Show your workouts, history, nutrition, measurements, and photos in the app  
- Play rest-timer sounds and show local reminders you turn on  
- Let you export a backup or CSV files you choose to share  

**We do not sell your personal information.**  
**We do not use your data for third-party advertising.**  
**We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.**

## 5. Sharing of information

We do not upload your gym data to our servers.

You may choose to share a **local backup file** or CSV export through the iOS share sheet. That copy is then under the service you pick.

We may see personal data only if you send it to support, or if required by law.

Apple (App Store, iOS) may process technical data under Apple’s privacy policy.

## 6. Data retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or delete Replio GymFit.

- Delete individual items in the app where that option exists  
- **Profile → Reset all data** wipes local app data and restores the default exercise library  
- Deleting the app removes local app storage on that device (unless you previously exported a backup, including iCloud device backups you enabled)

Backup files you saved outside the app remain until you delete them.

## 7. Security

Your data is stored in the app’s sandbox on the device. Protect your iPhone or iPad with a passcode / Face ID / Touch ID. No method of storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Children’s privacy

Replio GymFit is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, including Apple’s child-account rules). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Your choices

- Edit or delete workouts, logs, and photos in the app  
- Export or import a local backup from **Profile**  
- Turn off reminders in **Profile**  
- Control Camera, Photos, Notifications, and Microphone in iOS **Settings → Replio GymFit**  
- Reset or delete the app to remove local data  
- Email [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com) with privacy questions  

See also: [Data deletion]({{ site.baseurl }}/replio-gymfit/data-deletion/).

## 10. App Store privacy disclosures

This policy supports Apple App Store privacy policy requirements and App Privacy disclosures. Replio GymFit does not sell personal data and does not use your personal data for third-party advertising. Data you enter stays on device unless you export it yourself.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the updated version at this URL and change the “Last updated” date above.

## 12. Contact

Questions about privacy: [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)
