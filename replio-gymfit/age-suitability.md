# Age suitability — Replio GymFit

**Last updated:** 18 August 2026  
**App:** Replio GymFit  
**Recommended rating:** **Everyone / 4+** (utility fitness tracker; no objectionable media)  
**Support:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)

This page is for App Store age suitability and Google Play content-rating context.

## App overview

Replio GymFit is a personal gym log: workouts, rest timers, programs, nutrition, water, measurements, and optional progress photos. There is no social feed, no chat, no ads, and no user-generated public content.

## Content

| Category | Present? |
| --- | --- |
| Violence / weapons / horror | No |
| Sexual content / nudity | No (users may take their own progress photos; they stay on device) |
| Profanity | No |
| Alcohol, tobacco, drugs | No |
| Gambling / contests | No |
| Unrestricted web browsing | No |
| Third-party ads | No |

Fitness effort and body measurements are ordinary training tools, not medical treatment.

## Users

The App is a general-audience utility. It is **not directed at children under 13**. A parent or guardian should supervise any younger teen who uses a gym tracker. See the privacy policies for data practices (local storage; no developer account).

## Privacy and safety

- No public posting or social network  
- No location tracking  
- Progress photos stay on the device unless the user exports a backup
