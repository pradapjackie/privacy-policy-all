# Data deletion — Replio GymFit

**App:** Replio GymFit  
**Developer:** Pradap Pandiyan  
**Support:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)  
**App ID:** `com.pradappandiyan.gymworkout`

This page is for Google Play and App Store data-deletion requirements.

Replio GymFit **does not create an online account**. Workout data lives on your device. There is nothing for us to delete on a server.

## How to delete your data

### Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

1. Open **Replio GymFit**.  
2. Go to **Profile**.  
3. Tap **Reset all data** (or the equivalent reset control).  
4. Confirm. Local workouts, logs, photos stored by the app, and settings are wiped. The default exercise library is restored.

### Option 2 — Uninstall

Uninstalling Replio GymFit removes the app’s local storage on that device.

### Option 3 — Email

If you need help, email **support@pradappandiyan.com** with subject `Replio GymFit data deletion`.

Because we do not host accounts, email cannot wipe data still on your phone. Use in-app reset or uninstall for that.

## What is deleted

- Workouts, sessions, programs, and custom exercises  
- Nutrition, water, and measurement logs  
- Progress photos stored by the app  
- App settings and reminder schedule on that device  

## What may remain

- **Backup or CSV files** you previously exported (Files, Drive, email, AirDrop). Delete those copies yourself.  
- **Device backups** (Google backup / iCloud) may still contain app data until that backup is replaced.  
- **Emails** you sent to support, which we keep only as needed to answer you and then delete or minimize.

## After deletion

The app returns to a fresh install state on that device. Exporting a backup before reset is the only way to restore your logs.
