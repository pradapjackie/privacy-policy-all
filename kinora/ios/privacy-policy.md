# Privacy Policy — Kinora — Stay Close

**Last updated:** 5 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Kinora on **iOS** (`com.pradappandiyan.kinora`)  
**Also see:** [Android privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/kinora/android/privacy-policy/)

**Contact:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)  
**Operator:** Pradap Pandiyan (individual / sole developer)

This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use Kinora. Use this URL in App Store Connect.

> This document reflects how the product works today. It is not legal advice.

---

## 1. Who we are

Kinora (“Kinora — Stay Close”) is a relationship-care app. You add people you care about, set reminders to reach out, log calls and messages, keep notes and important dates, share family circles, and (on Premium) use AI-assisted suggestions and chat.

The app syncs your data to our cloud backend so you can sign in from your devices. There is also a web client for some features.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account

- Email address  
- Name (if you provide it)  
- Password (stored hashed; we do not keep your plain password)  
- Sign-in with **Google** (email and basic profile from the Google token)  
- Sign in with **Apple** (Apple may hide your email behind a private relay)  
- Session / device tokens needed to keep you logged in  
- Optional two-factor authentication settings  

### 2.2 Relationship data you create

- Contacts you add (names, relationship labels, notes, preferences)  
- Interaction logs (calls, messages, visits, custom events)  
- Important dates (birthdays, anniversaries, custom dates)  
- Memories / “things they told me” notes  
- Goals, gifts, life events you enter  
- Family Circles: circle names, members, invites, and circle chat messages  
- Reminder and suggestion settings  

### 2.3 Photos and media

- Contact avatar photos you upload  
- Album photos you attach to a person (subject to Free / Premium limits)  

### 2.4 Device permissions (only when you use the feature)

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | So you can pick people from your Contacts app to add as Kinora contacts. |
| **Photos / camera roll** | So you can set an avatar or add album photos. |
| **Location (when in use)** | Only if you choose a “use my location” action — not continuous tracking. |
| **Notifications** | Reminders to reach out. |
| **Calendar** | If you export dates to Calendar. |
| **Messages / Mail / WhatsApp** | Opens the system or third-party app to send a message you chose. |

### 2.5 AI features (Premium / trial)

If you use AI suggestions, message drafts, Assistant chat, or voice quick log, we process the text (and related contact context) through our backend and third-party AI providers.

### 2.6 Subscriptions and billing

Paid plans are sold through the App Store (and may be validated via RevenueCat). Card details are handled by Apple, not by us.

### 2.7 Technical logs

App version, device type / OS, crash or error logs, and diagnostics needed for reliability and security.

---

## 3. How we use information

- Provide and sync Kinora  
- Reminders and suggestions  
- Optional AI features you request  
- Authentication and Premium entitlements  
- Support and abuse prevention  

We do **not** sell your personal data or use your notes/photos for third-party advertising.

---

## 4. Sharing

Infrastructure hosts, AI providers (when you use AI), Apple / Google / RevenueCat for sign-in and billing, circle members you invite, and authorities when required by law.

---

## 5. Retention & deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. See [Account deletion]({{ site.baseurl }}/kinora/delete-account/) for how to erase your account. You can also delete many items inside the app without closing the account.

---

## 6. Security, children, international processing

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and access controls. Kinora is not directed at children under 13 (or the local minimum age). Data may be processed on servers outside your country.

---

## 7. Your choices & contact

Revoke permissions in iOS Settings, edit content in-app, or email [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com).
