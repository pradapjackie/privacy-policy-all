# Privacy Policy — Kinora — Stay Close

**Last updated:** 11 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Kinora on **Android** (`com.pradappandiyan.kinora`)  
**Also see:** [iOS privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/kinora/ios/privacy-policy/)

**Contact:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)  
**Operator:** Pradap Pandiyan (individual / sole developer)

This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use Kinora. Host this page at a public HTTPS URL for Google Play and App Store listings.

> This document reflects how the product works today. It is not legal advice.

---

## 1. Who we are

Kinora (“Kinora — Stay Close”) is a relationship-care app. You add people you care about, set reminders to reach out, log calls and messages, keep notes and important dates, share family circles, and (on Premium) use AI-assisted suggestions and chat.

The app syncs your data to our cloud backend so you can sign in from your devices. There is also a web client for some features.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account

- Email address  
- Name (if you provide it)  
- Password (stored hashed; we do not keep your plain password)  
- Sign-in with **Google** (email and basic profile from the Google token)  
- Sign in with **Apple** on iOS (Apple may hide your email behind a private relay)  
- Session / device tokens needed to keep you logged in  
- Optional two-factor authentication settings  

### 2.2 Relationship data you create

- Contacts you add (names, relationship labels, notes, preferences)  
- Interaction logs (calls, messages, visits, custom events)  
- Important dates (birthdays, anniversaries, custom dates)  
- Memories / “things they told me” notes  
- Goals, gifts, life events you enter  
- Family Circles: circle names, members, invites, and circle chat messages  
- Reminder and suggestion settings  

### 2.3 Photos and media

- Contact avatar photos you upload  
- Album photos you attach to a person (subject to Free / Premium limits)  
Photos are stored on our object storage so they can sync with your account.

### 2.4 Device permissions (only when you use the feature)

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | So you can pick people from your phone book to add as Kinora contacts. We do not upload your entire address book elsewhere without you choosing contacts to import. |
| **Photos / media** | So you can set an avatar or add album photos. |
| **Location (when in use)** | Only if you tap a “use my location” style action to find nearby contacts — not continuous tracking. |
| **Notifications** | Local / push reminders so you remember to reach out. |
| **Calendar** | If you export dates to your device calendar. |
| **SMS / WhatsApp / email (via system apps)** | Opens the native app to send a message you chose; we do not send SMS from our servers. |

### 2.5 AI features (Premium / trial)

Optional AI features (Assistant chat, conversation ideas, message drafts, and Quick Log) send selected text through Kinora’s servers to **OpenAI** so we can generate suggestions or parse what you typed.

**What may be sent:** messages you type to the assistant; contact names and relationship details; notes, memories, and life events you saved; recent interaction context; and Quick Log text you enter.

**Permission:** the app asks for your permission in-app before any AI feature shares this data with OpenAI. You can decline and keep using Kinora without AI features.

Data sent for AI is used only to power the feature you requested — not for advertising. Do not put secrets you would not trust a cloud AI with into those prompts.

### 2.6 Subscriptions and billing

Paid plans are sold through Google Play and/or the Apple App Store (and may be validated via RevenueCat). We receive entitlements (e.g. Free vs Premium) and related purchase metadata. **Card numbers are handled by Apple / Google**, not by us.

### 2.7 Technical logs

We may collect app version, device type / OS, crash or error logs, and coarse diagnostics needed to keep the service reliable and secure.

---

## 3. How we use information

- Provide and sync the Kinora service  
- Send reminders and in-app suggestions  
- Power optional AI features you request  
- Authenticate you (including Google / Apple sign-in)  
- Enforce Free / Premium limits  
- Respond to support requests  
- Protect against abuse and meet legal obligations  

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do **not** use your relationship notes or photos to show you third-party ads.

---

## 4. Sharing

We share data only with:

- **Infrastructure providers** that host our API, database, and file storage  
- **AI providers** when you use AI features (content needed to fulfill the request)  
- **Google / Apple / RevenueCat** for sign-in and subscriptions  
- **Authorities** if required by law  

People you invite to a **Family Circle** can see content shared in that circle according to how the feature works.

---

## 5. Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove or irreversibly anonymize personal data as described on our [account deletion page]({{ site.baseurl }}/kinora/delete-account/), subject to short-lived backups and legal requirements.

---

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (HTTPS, hashed passwords, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 7. Children

Kinora is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not create an account for a child below that age.

---

- Revoke OS permissions in system settings  
- Export / delete account where the app provides those controls
