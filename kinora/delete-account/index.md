# Account and data deletion — Kinora — Stay Close

**App:** Kinora — Stay Close  
**Package / bundle ID:** `com.pradappandiyan.kinora`  
**Developer:** Pradap Pandiyan  
**Support:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)

Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account or cloud data. Use one of the options below.

This page satisfies Google Play and App Store account-deletion requirements.

---

## Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

1. Sign in to Kinora.  
2. Open **Settings** (or **Account**).  
3. Choose **Delete account**.  
4. Confirm the warning. If asked for your password, enter it (leave blank only if you signed in with Google/Apple and never set a password).  
5. Confirm **Delete forever**.

Your profile, contacts, interactions, memories, important dates, photos, circles you own (as they relate to you), and related data are permanently deleted on our servers, and you are signed out.

---

## Option 2 — Email

1. Email **support@pradappandiyan.com** from the **same email** as your Kinora account.  
2. Subject: `Delete my Kinora account`  
3. State that you want the account and associated data deleted.

We verify ownership and process requests within **30 days** (usually sooner).

---

## What we delete

- Login (email, name, password hash, Google / Apple links, 2FA settings)  
- Contacts, interaction logs, memories, goals, gifts, life events, important dates  
- Avatars and album photos stored for your account  
- Family Circle memberships and circle content tied to you  
- Assistant / AI chat history tied to your user ID  
- Session tokens  

---

## What may be kept briefly

- **Database / storage backups** until the next backup cycle (typically days; not more than **90 days**)  
- **Purchase records** retained by Apple or Google under their policies  
- **Legal / fraud** records if the law requires retention  
- Limited **security logs** (then deleted or anonymized)

We do not keep your relationship data to sell or to use for ads.

---

## Delete some data without closing the account

Yes. While signed in you can delete individual contacts, photos, notes, dates, and other items in the app. That does **not** close the account.

---

## After deletion

You cannot sign in. Creating a new account with the same email starts empty — old data is not restored.
