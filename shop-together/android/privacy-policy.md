# Plan Mesh Privacy Policy (Android)

**Last updated:** 17 August 2026  
**Applies to:** Plan Mesh (formerly ShopTogether) on **Android** (Google Play) and the related Plan Mesh backend/API used by the Android app.

Plan Mesh (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a collaborative shopping-list app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Plan Mesh on Android, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Who we are

Plan Mesh is operated by Pradap Pandiyan.

**Contact / support:** [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Android app, we may collect:

### Account information
- Email address, username, name  
- Password (stored hashed; we do not store plain-text passwords)  
- Google Sign-In identifiers if you choose Google login  

### Profile preferences
- Optional country and currency settings  

### App content you create
- Shopping carts, items, quantities, notes, assignments, status updates  
- Cart chat messages and member roles  

### Media and device permissions you allow
- **Camera** — to scan QR codes when joining a cart  
- **Photos / media** — if you attach item photos  
- We do **not** use precise device location  

### Device and usage data
- Basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the service (for example request logs, error logs, timestamps, and app version)  
- Google Play / Android may also provide standard diagnostics according to your device settings  
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — crash and error reports (app version, device model, OS version, stack traces; not used for ads)  
- **Firebase Analytics** — aggregated in-app usage events to improve reliability and features (not sold; not used for third-party advertising)  

## 3. How we use information

We use this information to:

- Create and manage your account and authenticate you  
- Provide collaborative carts, item lists, member roles, sharing/join codes, and chat  
- Respond to support requests  
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability  

**We do not sell your personal information.**  
**We do not use your personal data for third-party advertising.**

## 4. Sharing of information

We may share information only as needed to run Plan Mesh:

- **Other cart members** — people you invite or who join your carts can see cart content, member names, and messages in that cart  
- **Service providers** — hosting and infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf (for example cloud hosting for the API and database)  
- **Legal requirements** — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security  

### Google services
If you use Google Sign-In or Google Play services, Google processes related data under Google’s privacy policy and terms.

## 5. Data retention

We keep account and app content while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account or content in the app or by contacting support. Some residual logs may be retained for a limited time for security and operational purposes.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Children’s privacy

Plan Mesh is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, including Google Play child-account rules). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- Update profile details in the app where available  
- Leave carts or stop using sharing features at any time  
- Control camera/photo permissions in Android **Settings → Apps → Plan Mesh → Permissions**  
- Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data in the app (**Profile → Delete account**) or by emailing support  

If you uninstall the Android app, server-side account data may remain until you request deletion.

## 9. International processing

Our servers and service providers may process data in countries other than where you live. By using Plan Mesh, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

## 10. Google Play disclosures

This policy supports Google Play **User Data** / privacy policy requirements. Plan Mesh does not sell personal data and does not use your personal data for third-party advertising.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL (or the privacy URL linked from the Google Play listing) and change the “Last updated” date above.

## 12. Contact

Questions about privacy: [support@pradappandiyan.com](mailto:support@pradappandiyan.com)
