# Plan Mesh — Account Deletion Request

**Developer:** Pradap Pandiyan  
**App:** Plan Mesh (formerly ShopTogether) — Google Play and App Store  
**Support:** support@pradappandiyan.com

Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account. Use one of the options below.

## Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

1. Sign in to Plan Mesh.
2. Open **Profile**.
3. Under **Manage**, tap **Delete account**.
4. Read the warning, tap **Continue**, type **DELETE**, then tap **Delete permanently**.
5. Your account and personal data are deleted on our servers, and you are signed out.

Deletion is completed in the app. We do not temporarily deactivate accounts.

## Option 2 — Email

1. Email **support@pradappandiyan.com** from the email address on your Plan Mesh account.
2. Subject: `Account deletion request`
3. Include your username and account email.

We will verify ownership and process your request within **30 days**.

## What we delete

- Account profile (name, username, email)
- Authentication credentials (password hash, Google / Apple sign-in links)
- Your shopping carts, items, and chat messages you created (and memberships)

## What we may retain briefly

- Limited security/server logs for up to **90 days** (then deleted or anonymized)
- Data we must keep if required by law
