# ShopTogether — Account Deletion Request

**Developer:** Pradap Pandiyan  
**App:** ShopTogether (Google Play)

## How to request deletion
1. Email **support@pradappandiyan.com** from the email address on your ShopTogether account.
2. Subject: `Account deletion request`
3. Include your username and account email.

We will verify ownership and process your request within **30 days**.

## What we delete
- Account profile (name, username, email)
- Authentication credentials
- Your shopping carts, items, and chat messages you created (and memberships)

## What we may retain briefly
- Limited security/server logs for up to **90 days** (then deleted or anonymized)
- Data we must keep if required by law

Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account. You must request deletion as above.
